Frequently Asked Questions

What is Keisen Smart Todo?
Keisen Smart Todo is a free collaborative Kanban board and task management tool. It features fully customizable columns with colors and sorting, drag-and-drop task management, team collaboration with 3 roles (Owner, Editor, Viewer), CFD analytics with bottleneck detection, subtasks with progress tracking, comments with images, file attachments, and cross-tool integrations with Eisenhower Matrix, Estimation Room and Agile Process.
Is Smart Todo free?
Yes, Keisen Smart Todo is free to use. You can create task lists, customize Kanban columns, invite team members, track analytics and export data. Sign in with Google and start managing tasks in seconds.
How does the Kanban board work?
Create a list and customize your columns: each column has a title, color, sorting rule (manual, by priority, by due date, by creation date) and an optional isDone flag that marks tasks in that column as completed. Default columns are To Do (blue), In Progress (orange) and Done (green, isDone=true). Add unlimited columns, reorder them, and drag tasks between columns. WIP is monitored in real time with aging detection.
What analytics does Smart Todo provide?
Smart Todo provides comprehensive Cumulative Flow Diagram (CFD) analytics: Lead Time (average, median, p85, p95 with trend comparison), Cycle Time metrics, Throughput (daily and weekly averages with trend), WIP monitoring with aging detection (ok/watch/aging thresholds), Flow health (arrival vs completion rates with status), Bottleneck detection with severity levels, and Team workload balance analysis with per-member distribution.
Can I collaborate with my team?
Yes. Smart Todo supports 3 participant roles: Owner (full control over list settings, columns and members), Editor (can create, edit and move tasks), and Viewer (read-only access). Invite team members via email with automatic deep link support. All changes are tracked in a detailed audit trail with field-level change tracking.
What cross-tool integrations are available?
Smart Todo integrates with 3 other Keisen tools: export tasks to the Eisenhower Matrix for urgency/importance prioritization, send tasks to the Estimation Room for team estimation sessions (Fibonacci, T-Shirt, PERT, etc.), or convert tasks into User Stories for the Agile Process Manager. You can also export to CSV and Google Sheets for external reporting.
Can I track task history and changes?
Yes. Smart Todo includes a comprehensive audit trail tracking 12 actions (create, update, delete, archive, restore, move, assign, invite, join, revoke, reorder, batch create) across 6 entity types (list, task, invite, participant, column, tag). Every change records field-level detail showing previous and new values. Filter the audit log by entity type, action, user, date range and tags.
Does Smart Todo work on mobile devices?
Yes, Keisen Smart Todo is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and desktop browsers. You can manage tasks, move cards on the Kanban board, and check analytics from any device — no app download needed. The interface adapts to your screen size for the best experience on every device.
How does Keisen compare to Trello or Todoist?
Unlike Trello (limited to 10 boards and 10 members on the free plan, no built-in analytics) or Todoist (5 projects and 5 collaborators free, no Kanban view), Keisen Smart Todo offers unlimited custom columns, built-in CFD analytics with bottleneck detection, a 12-action audit trail with field-level detail, cross-tool integrations (Eisenhower, Estimation, Agile), team workload analysis, and batch import/export — all free. It's a complete task management ecosystem, not just a board.
