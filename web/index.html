Frequently Asked Questions

What is Keisen?
Keisen is an all-in-one agile platform inspired by the Kaizen philosophy of continuous improvement. It includes 5 integrated tools: Smart Todo with Kanban board, Eisenhower Matrix for prioritization, Estimation Room with 7 estimation modes, Agile Process Manager for Scrum/Kanban/Hybrid frameworks, and Retrospective Board with 6 templates. It works entirely in the browser with no installation required.
What does Keisen mean? Is it related to Kaizen?
The name Keisen is inspired by Kaizen (改善), the Japanese philosophy of continuous improvement born in the Toyota Production System. Keisen applies Kaizen principles through modern agile tools: Retrospective Boards drive systematic team reflection and actionable improvements, Kanban boards with WIP limits enable continuous flow optimization, and the integrated workflow encourages teams to improve with every iteration. While Keisen uses Western agile frameworks like Scrum and Kanban, its core mission — helping teams get better every day — is rooted in Kaizen.
Is Keisen really free?
Yes. The Free plan includes 5 entities per tool, 50 tasks per entity, and 10 invites. For larger teams, the Premium plan (EUR 4.99/month or EUR 39.99/year) offers 30 entities and 100 tasks. The Elite plan (EUR 7.99/month or EUR 69.99/year) offers unlimited resources and priority support.
What tools does Keisen include?
Smart Todo & Kanban Board with custom columns, WIP limits, and CFD analytics. Eisenhower Matrix with voting and RACI matrix. Estimation Room with 7 modes (Fibonacci, T-Shirt, PERT, Dot Voting, Bucket, Decimal, Five Fingers). Agile Process Manager with Scrum, Kanban, and Hybrid. Retrospective Board with 6 templates (Start/Stop/Continue, Sailboat, 4Ls, Starfish, Mad/Sad/Glad, DAKI).
How does Keisen compare to Trello, Todoist, or Asana?
Unlike Trello (10 boards, 10 members free, no WIP limits or analytics), Todoist (5 projects, no customizable Kanban, no CFD), or Asana (10 teammates, audit log Enterprise-only), Keisen offers WIP limits, CFD analytics, audit trail, and 5 integrated tools on the free tier. No other free platform combines Kanban, Eisenhower, Planning Poker, Sprint Management, and Retrospective boards.
Do I need to install anything?
No. Keisen is a web application that works entirely in the browser. No downloads, no installations, no configuration needed. Fully responsive on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers — accessible from any device with a modern web browser.
Can I collaborate with my team?
Yes. Invite team members via email. Each tool supports role-based permissions (Owner, Editor, Viewer). Agile projects add Scrum roles (Product Owner, Scrum Master, Developer, Stakeholder). Real-time updates keep everyone synchronized.
What languages does Keisen support?
Keisen is available in English, Italian, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, German, and Indonesian. The interface, methodology guides (Scrum, Kanban, Hybrid), and retrospective templates are fully localized in all languages.
Is my data secure?
Yes. Keisen uses Firebase (Google Cloud) infrastructure with enterprise-grade security. Authentication via Google Account or email/password. GDPR compliant with privacy policy, data management, and right to erasure.
